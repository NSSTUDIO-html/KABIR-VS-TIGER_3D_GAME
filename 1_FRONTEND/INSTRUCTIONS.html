🐅 Kabir vs Tiger 3D

Game instructions · AI Mode

Characters

Kabir

Hrithik Roshan

Tiger

Jr NTR

AI Mode – two ways to play

  • You play as Kabir, the AI plays Tiger
  • You play as Tiger, the AI plays Kabir

Health

Both fighters start with 100% blood HP.

The 5 weapons

The opponent tells you to unlock each weapon by solving riddles of one subject:

  1. 1Maths
  2. 2Physics
  3. 3Chemistry
  4. 4Coding
  5. 5English grammar

Unlock rule

Both fighters, the human player and the AI, unlock weapons the same way. If the player unlocks some of the 5 weapons, the opponent unlocks the same number out of 5.

Controls

  • 4 move buttons to move
  • 1 shoot button for each fighter

🤫 Secret

To defeat the AI opponent completely, swipe along the weapon bar from the first weapon to the last. All 5 weapons unlock at once.
